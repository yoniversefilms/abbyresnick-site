The Studio

A florist's hands, a growing studio

Abby Resnick Floral Design began at a single worktable in Los Angeles, gathering flowers by hand for the people she knew. Today that table is a studio — a small, devoted team that brings the same intimate care to larger weddings, multi-day celebrations, and full-scale installations.

We design, source, build, and style on site, so every event arrives feeling considered down to the last stem — whether it's a quiet dinner for ten or a celebration for three hundred.

— Abby
