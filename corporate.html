Procurement & Planning

Questions, answered

The details planners, DMCs, and procurement teams ask first.

What lead times do you need? +
We prefer four to six weeks for larger activations, installations, and multi-day programs, which gives us time to source specialty product and confirm load-in. Smaller dinners and single focal moments can often be turned faster — reach out with your date and we will tell you honestly whether we can deliver.
Can you match our brand colors or Pantone? +
Yes. Share your brand guidelines or Pantone references and we design the palette to match, selecting blooms, foliage, and finishes that hold true under event lighting and on camera. Where a flower cannot hit an exact shade, we propose the closest natural match plus accents to bridge the gap.
Do you handle install and strike? +
Always. Our team builds on site to your schedule, coordinates with your venue and production crew, and breaks everything down cleanly after the event — including disposal and removal. Your team never has to touch the florals or manage the load-out.
Is there a minimum? +
Every program is quoted to the brief — scope, scale, and venue all move the number. Share your budget range in the inquiry form and we will tell you honestly what is achievable, then give you an itemized proposal so you can see exactly where it goes.
Do you travel? +
We serve all of greater Los Angeles, and travel beyond LA — including destination programs — is available for the right project. Travel, lodging, and logistics are quoted transparently as a line item so there are no surprises.
Are you insured? Can you be added as additional insured? +
Yes. We carry general liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance (COI), and we routinely name venues, properties, and partners as additional insured to meet their requirements. We are comfortable working within standard venue and DMC vendor requirements — send us your venue’s COI requirements with your inquiry and we will have documentation ready ahead of load-in.
